Speech and language impairments following neurological damage, developmental conditions, or neurodegenerative diseases often require intensive and repeated rehabilitation. Although technology-based and home-based interventions are increasingly available, current approaches provide limited objective multimodal feedback (mostly a numerical score) and personalized real-time adaptation.
Moreover, access to specialist rehabilitation services remains challenging in rural and resource-constrained settings. These limitations motivate the development of an affordable, privacy-preserving, real-time rehabilitation system deployable beyond clinical environments, enhanced by a local LLM providing personalized natural-language feedback. The expected impact is more accessible, personalized home rehabilitation and longitudinal monitoring, with sensitive data processed locally and reduced dependence on reliable connectivity.

The project starts on January 1st, 2027. More details to come.
