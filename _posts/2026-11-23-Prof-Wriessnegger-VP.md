The WavesLab will welcome **Prof. Selina Wriessnegger from the Technical University of Graz (Austria)** as a Visiting Professor from November 23rd to December 4th.

During her stay, she will held two classes to our students of *Computational Neuroscience* in the **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DSAI)** Master of Science Degree. Classes will cover the most important principles for designing an effective Brain-Computer Interface, and different types of BCIs with examples (active, reactive and passive).

On November, 26th she will also give a seminar on *Passive Brain-Computer Interfaces in EEG-based neuroadaptive systems* and take part in the following round table (see related news).


The WavesLab wants to thank the Department of Mathematics, Informatics, and Geosciences and the University of Trieste for supporting this Visiting Professor opportunity.
