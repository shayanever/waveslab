The WavesLab is excited to announce that **Prof. Selina Wriessnegger from the Technical University of Graz (Austria)** will be Visiting Professor from November 23r to December 4th.

On November, 26th she will give a seminar on *Passive Brain-Computer Interfaces in EEG-based neuroadaptive systems*. Following, a round table will take place with other two guests, [Dr. Luisa Cacciante](https://hsancamillo.it/luisa-cacciante/) (speech therapist from IRCCS San Camillo of Venice) and [Prof. Pietro Aricò](https://phd.uniroma1.it/web/PIETRO-ARIC%C3%92'_nC4381_IT.aspx) (Associate Professor in Bioengineering at Sapienza University).

The event is free and open to everyone, just upon registration (a registration form will be soon available).
